Brand Guidelines

Logo, colour, type, voice, the full website design system, and every applied surface. Written to be implemented, not admired — all tokens ship in buloo-tokens.css.

v1.0 · for engineers & design partners Bahasa Indonesia first, English secondary Every size of petshop

01 — Foundation

Friendly is the tone. Cute is the failure.

Buloo holds the money, the schedule, and the medical history of animals people love. That has to feel safe. But the person using it is a groomer with a wet dog in one arm — not an operations manager — and a good share of our customers are one person working out of their house.

Who it's for

A solo home-groomer with fifteen regulars, and a five-branch chain. The brand reads identically to both.

What it replaces

A WhatsApp group, a whiteboard, a cash drawer, and the notebook where the vaccination dates live.

The promise

Nothing about a pet gets forgotten — not the allergy, not the booking, not the vaccine due next month.

Personality

Warm

Talks like a colleague at the counter. Never baby talk.

Capable

Numbers and dates are always exact. Never cold.

Reachable

No jargon, no enterprise posturing. Never simplistic.

Light

One wink per surface. Never silly.

The cute line

This brand fails in one specific direction: too cute. Concretely — no faces on objects, no paw prints as bullet points, no more than one emoji anywhere, no diminutives in product copy, no mascot. Warmth comes from the roundness of the shapes and the plainness of the language, not from decoration. When a choice is between charming and clear, pick clear.

The "any level" rule. Never describe customers by their size. No "UMKM", no "bisnis skala besar", no tier language in copy or on pricing pages. A petshop is a petshop.

02 — Logo

One ear. That's the whole idea.

The wordmark is clean geometry — circles, one stroke weight, round caps — interrupted by a single soft ear beside the b. The ear is deliberately unspecified: dog, cat, rabbit. It belongs to whatever animal walks into the shop, which is the point. The final o lifts to orange because a brand needs one place where it stops behaving.

Primary — works at every size. There is no longer a separate small-size wordmark.

Construction

All values in the SVG's own units. These are fixed — do not eyeball a rebuild.

Plain — sparkles dropped. Use below 140 px wide, in dense UI, and on anything printed at one colour.

Reversed — on navy, on photography, on dark UI. The accent o stays orange.

Rules

  • Clear space — the height of one o on all four sides, measured from the ear on the left and the sparkles on top.
  • Minimum size — 140 px wide with sparkles, 96 px without. Below 96 px, use an icon instead of the wordmark.
  • Orange lives on the last o only. Never on another letter, never on the ear, never on all of them.
  • Single colour — for embroidery, stamps, and one-colour print, use buloo-logo-mono.svg. Never approximate by hand.

Never

✕ Put anything inside the b's counter — no face, no icon
✕ Close, widen, or mirror the ear gap
✕ Let the two os touch or overlap
✕ Stretch, skew, rotate, or outline any part
✕ Add shadows, glows, or gradients
✕ Retype it in a font — it is drawn geometry

03 — Icons

Navy is the icon. The others have jobs.

Three variants exist and each has exactly one purpose. Two competing silhouettes is the fastest way to dilute recognition, so the navy squircle is the icon people should learn — everything else is situational.

Primary — app icon, favicon, PWA, WhatsApp Business avatar, struk header

Alternate — social avatars only, where the crop is round anyway. Never as the app icon.

Mono — embroidery on aprons and kaos, rubber stamps, engraving, fax-grade print

The ear survives all the way to 20 px, which is why nothing else needed to go inside the letter.

Export sizes engineers will need

Favicon 16 / 32 / 48 (ICO) · PWA 192 / 512 PNG + 512 maskable · iOS 180 · Android adaptive 432 with the mark inside the 264 safe circle · Open Graph 1200 × 630 using the reversed wordmark on navy, not the icon.

Why orange isn't the app icon

White on orange measures about 2.1:1. Fine on a social platform where the avatar sits on a fixed background, but on a phone home screen — over a bright wallpaper, next to a dozen other icons — it loses definition and reads as an unfinished brand. Navy holds at 20 px. Orange does not.

04 — Colour

Navy earns the trust. Orange means act.

Navy is the working colour — money screens, records, reports. Orange appears rarely and always signals something a human must do: sign up, pay, respond to a due date. That discipline is what keeps "friendly" from sliding into "unserious".

Orange never exceeds about 5% of a screen. As a background or a header bar it turns the brand into a discount flyer and shop owners stop trusting it with money.

⚠ The one accessibility trap

Orange #F5921E on white measures ~2.1:1 and fails WCAG for text. Three non-negotiable consequences: orange is a fill, never a text colour on white; orange buttons carry Deep Navy labels, not white; and for orange text on light surfaces use #B96A05 instead.

05 — Typography

Plus Jakarta Sans & Inter.

Plus Jakarta Sans was commissioned for the city of Jakarta's identity and drawn by an Indonesian foundry — genuinely local without looking it. Its geometric bowls echo the logo directly, and it carries warmth without the rounded terminals that tip a brand into cute. Inter takes body copy, interface labels, and every number: it survives 13 px on a cheap Android under fluorescent light, and its tabular figures keep rupiah columns aligned. Both free on Google Fonts.

06 — Voice

Bahasa Indonesia, spoken like a colleague.

Buloo is not translated software. It thinks in Bahasa and borrows English only where the trade already does — grooming, booking, member. Four rules: talk, don't lecture. Name real things. Keep it short, their hands are full. Never blame the user.

Appearing on someone else's surface

On a shop's struk, booking page, or receipt, Buloo appears as text only — "Dicatat dengan Buloo" or "Powered by Buloo", set in Inter Medium at no more than 11 px, in Slate. No logo, no icon, no colour. The shop's brand is the one the customer came for; ours is a footnote until the relationship justifies more.

Bayar & kirim struk

The product runs on white and Cloud, not navy. A groomer looks at this forty times a day under fluorescent light — the brand colour belongs in the nav bar and the buttons, not the canvas.

Icons, illustration, motion

  • Icons — Lucide or Phosphor, rounded caps, 2 px stroke at 24 px. Never mix sets; never mix filled and outline.
  • Illustration — flat, no outlines, built from circles and capsules like the letterforms. Navy and Sky base, orange for one focal element per drawing. No faces on objects.
  • Photography — real Indonesian petshops, natural light, visible grooming tables and clutter. Never a golden retriever on a white cyclorama.
  • Motion — 160 ms ease-out for micro-interactions, 240 ms for panels, nothing over 400 ms. The logo's only sanctioned animation is the three sparkles fading up in sequence over 240 ms on first load, once per session. Honour prefers-reduced-motion.

Accessibility floor

  • Body text ≥ 15 px, contrast ≥ 4.5:1. Deep Navy on white and white on navy both clear it.
  • Orange is never a text colour on white.
  • Visible focus ring on every interactive element. Touch targets ≥ 44 × 44 px.
  • Status is never colour alone — every coloured badge pairs with a word.

  • 58 mm = 384 px printable at 203 dpi. 80 mm = 576 px.
  • Pure 1-bit black. No greys, no anti-aliasing, no colour — thermal printers dither everything else into mush.
  • Use the mono icon only, max 200 px wide (58 mm) or 300 px (80 mm). Never the wordmark with sparkles.
  • Use the printer's built-in font for all text. Don't try to render Plus Jakarta Sans as a bitmap.
  • Footer line is always "Dicatat dengan Buloo" — small, centred, never above the shop's own name.

WhatsApp & social

  • WhatsApp Business avatar — navy squircle icon (it renders as a circle; the squircle survives the crop).
  • Social avatars — orange circle icon. This is its only use.
  • Instagram post 1080 × 1080, story 1080 × 1920, 80 px safe margins.
  • One orange element per post. Headline max two lines, Plus Jakarta Sans 800.
  • Message templates carry at most one 🐾 and only when they reach a pet owner.

Signage, stickers & merch

  • Window sticker 100 × 100 mm die-cut circle, orange icon, text "Booking di sini pakai Buloo".
  • Spanduk — reversed wordmark on navy, logo width ≥ 25% of banner width.
  • Apron & kaos — mono icon embroidered, minimum 40 mm wide. Embroidery cannot hold the 18 px ear gap below that.
  • Rubber stamp — mono icon only, minimum 25 mm.

09 — The short version

Navy does the work. Orange does the smile. One smile per screen.

Round everything. Nothing goes inside the b. Keep the ear gap. Write like a colleague, in Bahasa. And never describe a customer by how big their shop is — the one-person groomer and the five-branch chain get exactly the same brand.
